# 0004. Redirects are `302` with `no-store`, never `301`

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-06
- **Spec:** §2 (anti-goals), §6

## Context

A click is counted only if the request reaches the Worker. Links can also expire, and the expiry is only enforced if
the request reaches the Worker.

## Decision

A working link answers `302 Found`, with `Location: <target>` and `Cache-Control: private, no-store`.

## Why not the alternatives

- **`301 Moved Permanently` (or `308`):** browsers may cache a permanent redirect indefinitely. Repeat clicks then go
  straight to the target: they are never counted, and they keep working after the link expires.
- **`302` without `no-store`:** a browser or a shared cache may still reuse it. `private, no-store` rules out both.
- **`307`:** works just as well for `GET`. `302` is the conventional choice for link shorteners and what readers
  expect.

## Consequences

- Every click costs one Worker request. That is the point: it's how we count it.
- Expired links reliably get the "This link has expired" page (`410`), even for someone who clicked before.

## Revisit when

Never for counted links. A future "permanent, uncounted" link type would be a new decision.
